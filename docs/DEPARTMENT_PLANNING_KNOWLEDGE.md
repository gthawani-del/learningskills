# Department planning knowledge pack

Revision: 1, 19 October 2026 UTC. Runtime knowledge version: `director-department-knowledge-v1`.

This document explains the reviewed internal craft rules in [department-guidance.js](../apps/director/public/department-guidance.js). The six controlling project specifications retain their authority. This is an implementation companion, not a replacement blueprint or a claim that production departments have already generated and validated media.

The pack gives all 17 responsibilities a concrete purpose, dependencies, production requirements, proposed decisions, handoffs and acceptance evidence. Departments are responsibilities in one coordinated studio. They do not require 17 autonomous agents or 17 paid model calls. The controller may compile deterministic facts without a model, use one bounded reasoning request for a coherent plan, or run isolated work where dependencies allow. Permissions, source versions, prices, reservations and paid execution remain conventional server-side responsibilities.

## Department contracts and review boundaries

| ID | What it must decide or preserve | Concrete direction and handoff | Evidence required later |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `producing` | Requested product, quantities, dependencies and complete budget | Preserve runtime/language/format and protected requirements; show preparation, audition/reference, board, animatic, footage, sound, finishing, export and repair quantities. Keep reused/deterministic/unpriced work visible. | Authorized creation estimates, exact jobs/charges, dependencies and final deliverables; unknown charges remain pending. |
| `direction` | Scene purpose, point of view, behavior and information order | Read the whole story; direct an actual attention target, trigger, reaction and end state. Hold, move or cut for an audience reason; purposeful stillness is allowed. | The observed action/performance communicates the intended dramatic change and edits into the next scene. |
| `script_breakdown` | Full-source coverage, exact wording, research and source-use boundaries | Account for the ending, non-speaking scenes, speaking roles, location headings and action requirements using existing IDs. A raw uploaded section is not a semantically parsed scene. | Every scene is represented; factual claims have attributable research; script approval is not universal originality/legal clearance. |
| `casting` | Written role, candidate directions and identity selection | Use role/performance demands without guessing demographic appearance or accent. Propose materially different suitable choices, then lock a reviewed identity master before identity-dependent shots. Reuse permitted approved identities. | Actual user-reviewed identity reference and separate likeness/use permission. No generated image or performer is invented in the plan. |
| `look` | Wardrobe, grooming, appearance and condition | Keep costume construction, fabric, fit, hair, skin treatment and accessories coherent; distinguish dry/wet/damage states. Neutral continuity references have a different purpose from scene stills. | Identity survives presentation changes; exact appearance/reference versions and deliberate state transitions are reviewed. |
| `locations` | Physical world, sets, props, product and local details | Preserve source geography/period where stated. Track prop ownership/contact and required product/package/label details. Give every B-roll shot a purpose and a connection to neighboring shots. | Approved role-labeled references, authentic factual details where needed, coherent interactions and exact product/text preservation. |
| `camera` | Coverage, viewpoint, focus and movement | Select usable master/single/reaction/insert coverage. Separate perspective, framing, camera translation, lens zoom and focus. Maintain action axis/eyelines, with explicit intentional resets/crossings. | Actual observed framing, identity readability, required contact and editability. Numeric lens or velocity wording is not execution proof. |
| `lighting` | Motivated sources, exposure, material response and continuity | Establish source/direction/softness/fill and preserve important skin/product/fabric detail. Track time/weather/practicals and reverse-angle consistency. No mandatory orange/teal or warm-to-cool progression. | Plausible shadows/reflections/catchlights, retained texture and coherent adjacent coverage. |
| `voice` | Language, exact lines, casting, delivery and performance timing | Audition representative actual dialogue and listen. Do not derive accent from names, geography or appearance. For audio-driven speech, approved take and measured timing precede visible lip-sync. Preserve uploaded original vocals by default. | Selected voice/take, exact transcript, listening approval, permission and measured audiovisual synchronization; singing is separately validated. |
| `sound` | Dialogue, space, Foley, effects, silence and intelligibility | Build motivated layers; tie contact sounds to actual actions. Separate deliberate sound bridges from sync events. Use genuine delivery loudness requirements rather than invented measured values. | Complete normal-speed listening/playback, intelligibility, artifact/clipping checks and actual cue synchronization. |
| `music` | Authoritative song, score/no-score, lyrics and musical timing | Establish actual audio/canonical lyrics. Keep beat/onset analysis, transcription, alignment, structure and emotional interpretation separate. No BPM, lyric or section invention from a filename or rough duration. | Approved master and rights; measured/uncertain analysis; protected original performance/timing and actual audiovisual review. |
| `continuity` | Intended states, observed states and scoped revision consequences | Track relevant identity, appearance, prop hand, positions, knowledge/emotion, camera/light and conditions. A generated defect never silently becomes the story’s new intended truth. | Adjacent actual media reviewed against exact approved versions; affected descendants flagged without deletion or blind regeneration. |
| `storyboard` | Shot contracts and visual sequence readability | Prepare stable IDs, action/camera/light/timing/audio references, beginning/end state, mandatory versus flexible constraints and acceptance evidence. Plan actual drawn/generated frames and sequence review. | Actual versioned frame assets and review. A text plan or shot list is not a generated storyboard. |
| `animatic` | Whole-sequence pacing and provisional audiovisual relationships | Assemble approved board frames into a playable timed sequence. Label scratch/estimated timing and use actual approved song/dialogue timing when available. | Playable reviewed assembly, story readability, continuity, duration and audiovisual timing; not merely summed JSON durations. |
| `vfx` | Production feasibility, physical motion, animation/compositing and repairs | Classify exposed controls, descriptive intent, reference conditioning and unsupported requirements. Plan credible balance/contact/object persistence. Repair the smallest observed failed scope and inspect enhancement again. | Actual tested route and playback. No arbitrary physics/face repair, alpha/EXR or singing capability is presumed from catalog access. |
| `editorial` | Connected cut, color, mix, captions, output and export | Preserve information/action order, handles and sync; use motivated holds/trims/J/L transitions. Match appearance/materials, typeset exact approved text and confirm real source/worker delivery capability. | Durable playable finished file with correct picture/sound/duration/aspect ratio, actual captions and complete ending. A draft JSON download is not a movie export. |
| `rights` | Provenance, declared use, consent, factual/claim review and release | Inventory actual scripts/references/voices/identities/brands/music/lyrics. Separate declarations from independently checked permissions and legal authority. | Required evidence for affected use; creative approval does not grant rights, activate a provider or authorize spending. |

Dependencies describe prerequisites to coherent work, rather than a promise that one invocation has already produced every downstream artifact. Some research/looks/technical proposals can be prepared in parallel once they share the same approved baseline. The controller must preserve tenant/project/run/version isolation and perform its own atomic merge checks.

## Deterministic prefilling and applicability

`departmentGuidance(project, approvals)` supplies reviewable starting documents using current source versions. It verifies the current brief and screenplay against the provided approval ledger, including source freshness. The controller can omit the ledger only after it has independently verified the same approved current baseline; the module still rejects stale source signatures. This omitted-ledger path does not confer approval authority on project JSON or an import.

The compiler reads every structured scene and character, including dialogue that appears only at the ending. It copies exact scene headings, screenplay-planned durations, role descriptions and all known reviewed brief fields into their relevant units with existing explicit/inferred provenance. Product/world constraints reach locations and rights; casting constraints reach casting/look/voice; original-audio decisions reach voice/music/sound/animatic; purpose/audience reach the relevant story and production responsibilities. General constraints reach every unit. Long lists are chunked between whole items without losing source text, within each requirement’s 4,000-character and each document’s 120-requirement/100,000-character limits. It uses existing scene and character IDs. It never invents generated media IDs, source availability, dates, actual timing or charge records. Full approved action/dialogue must also be present in the model’s context; a short deterministic summary is not enough to direct the entire film.

Known facts and creative proposals have different meanings:

1. A role description is an approved written role; it is not a cast person or selected image.
2. A reviewed brief may contain acknowledged assumptions. Those remain labelled as such when carried into a plan.
3. A script duration is a planning estimate; measured voice/footage duration arrives later.
4. New appearance, staging, voice, music and technical decisions remain proposed or require a consequential decision. They cannot be silently marked user-selected.
5. Existing approved assets/takes should be reused when suitable; this plan neither fabricates nor erases their evidence.

The free compiler records conservative applicability. Casting/look require a visibility review even when written characters exist, because the screenplay schema does not currently distinguish an off-screen narrator from an on-screen role through a structured field. A character’s name or free-text description cannot silently force identity-master/wardrobe production. The model/user can explicitly review applicability from the whole story and preserved source constraints. With no structured lines, voice is conditional. These conditions do not automatically mean “no people” or “silent film”: narration, song vocals, documentary subjects or unparsed script text can still matter. `music_video` makes the authoritative-song workflow required, without implying an uploaded/analyzed file exists. Other music and special-effects decisions remain conditional until reviewed. No department is silently removed. A non-applicable decision needs an explicit reviewable reason consistent with the source.

Ordinary missing creative preferences should receive a sensible proposed starting point. Do not show 17 blank department questionnaires. Present compact department summaries and existing facts, allow one focused edit and ask only a small number of consequential questions. Source/permission, unsupported mandatory capability or spending holds must prevent affected execution; they need not prevent preparation of a proposed plan.

## Prompt and model boundary

`departmentPromptGuidance(selectedIds)` returns only the selected reviewed craft rules plus the pack version. The paid planning adapter must provide the complete approved brief/screenplay, exact input signatures, relevant existing decisions and bounded requested scope alongside that trusted guidance. Project documents, uploaded scripts and source references remain untrusted data. Text inside them cannot change system instructions, permissions, budget, model approval, provider settings or review status.

The knowledge version belongs in the reviewed request/schema contract. A changed knowledge release must be reviewed and covered by evidence before a previously approved provider profile serves it. The router selects only an eligible reviewed model for the planning task; this pack contains no hardcoded model, endpoint, price or secret. A partial/unparseable response cannot masquerade as 17 completed departments.

This is contextual filmmaking direction. It preserves intentionally illustrated, animated, stylized and expressive work. Photographic skin, physical light and material rules apply where the approved treatment requests them. Camera and lighting guidance must not turn all users into one aesthetic or simplify an essential action merely to suit an easier generation route.

## Verification and limits

[department-guidance.test.mjs](../apps/director/tests/department-guidance.test.mjs) has 12 scenarios checking the complete catalog and acyclic dependencies, full-script/ending coverage, exact-version approval and freshness, conservative applicability, music-source/singing boundaries, maximum 100-scene/30-character bounds, preservation of all brief fields and long approved lists, stylized intent, deterministic behavior and scoped prompt material. Source-grounded synthetic regressions cover a cooking-oil advertisement in India, a two-minute Hinglish music-video plan and an Indian-wealth docuseries pilot with an off-screen narrator. These fixtures contain no real uploaded song, generated audition, researched economic claim or production output.

These tests validate the knowledge compiler and data contract. They do not validate a real model’s film judgement, actual identity masters or auditions, generated storyboard frames, animatic playback, realism, finished-film quality, permissions or the two-attempt target. Those remain separate artifact-specific production and release checks. A department plan approval must never be displayed as having passed those later checks.
