[Official H3 lip-sync guide](https://fal.ai/h3-max-lip-sync) describes 5–15-second inputs, clipping above 15 seconds and transcription enabled by default. The live schema instead describes up to 15 minutes and defaults transcription off. Both expose a transcription control; the guide explicitly discusses disabling it for singing. This supports investigating singing, not a quality guarantee. Do not send a full song until duration behavior, cost and audio preservation are verified. Set any accepted boolean explicitly rather than relying on a default. Preserve the canonical master and segment on musical/lyrical boundaries only under an approved plan.

[Higgsfield's official ownership help](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/account/who-owns-my-generations-and-can-i-use-them-commercially) distinguishes commercial rights from model-training use. Its stated standard terms permit training use; enterprise terms differ. Therefore the supplied video's broad ownership/privacy summary cannot establish a no-training promise. This does not authorize adding Higgsfield as a processing provider to our fal/OpenAI stack.
